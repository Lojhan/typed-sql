# Code of conduct

typed-sql contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and free from
harassment. Disagreement about design is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language,
threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publication of private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
security-advisory channel. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and public representation
of the project.
